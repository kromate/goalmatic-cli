---
title: Cost and limits
description: Keep development within the approved zero-spend budget.
---

This project has a hard zero-spending constraint. Do not buy a Mintlify plan, npm service, domain, or other paid resource for this work.

Mintlify’s free Starter plan is the intended documentation host. Confirm current terms on the [Mintlify pricing page](https://www.mintlify.com/pricing) before creating an account or changing hosting. Hosting docs does not prove that the CLI or Goalmatic endpoints are released.

The local Vue demo does not require Goalmatic credits. An authenticated App can use account-scoped resources and shared credits when its product contract permits an operation. Platform quotas, account limits, and any operation costs still apply. The CLI must show a denial or credit-limit error and stop.

Do not put private Goalmatic credentials, provider keys, or service-account files in the starter, docs, CI logs, or repository history.
